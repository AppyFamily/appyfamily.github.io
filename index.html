App 01 — Nozey

Know what's happening
on your own street.

Nozey turns official UK police crime data into a map you can actually use. No spreadsheets, no jargon — just tap a marker near you and see what was reported, when, and what happened next.

  • 1 Browse an interactive map built on real UK police data
  • 2 Tap any marker to see crime type, location, and current status
  • 3 Get a clearer picture of safety in your local area

Why "Appy Family"

Small apps, built with care, one at a time.

Appy Family started with a simple idea: build the kind of apps you'd actually want a family member to use — straightforward, useful, and honest about what they do. Nozey is the first house on the street. Each new app gets built the same way: pick one real problem, solve it well, and give it room to grow.

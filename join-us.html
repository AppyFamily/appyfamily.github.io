THE OPPORTUNITY

Co-Founder — Marketing & Growth

📍 Remote / UK 🚀 Early-stage startup 🤝 Co-Founder opportunity ⏱ Flexible

Help us turn good ideas into products people know about.

Appy Family is building practical apps and digital products designed to solve everyday problems. We’re looking for a commercially minded, creative co-founder who can take ownership of the marketing and growth side of the business.

You won’t simply be joining to manage a few social media accounts. We’re looking for somebody who wants to help shape the brand, build an audience, develop our go-to-market strategy and grow Appy Family alongside the founder.

YOUR ROLE

What You’ll Own

You’ll lead the marketing and growth side of Appy Family, working alongside the founder to take our products from launch to real users.

📱

Social Media & Content

Own our social media presence, create engaging content and develop a consistent voice across the Appy Family brand and our products.

📈

Growth & User Acquisition

Find creative, low-cost ways to attract users, test new marketing channels and turn awareness into downloads, sign-ups and customers.

🚀

Product Launches

Help plan and execute go-to-market campaigns for new Appy Family products, from early validation through to launch and growth.

🤝

Partnerships & Outreach

Identify creators, communities, organisations and potential partners who can help us reach the right audiences.

💡

Brand & Strategy

Help shape how Appy Family and its products are positioned, communicated and differentiated as the business develops.

🧪

Experiment & Learn

Test ideas, measure what works and continuously improve our marketing rather than relying on assumptions.

ABOUT YOU

What We’re Looking For

We care more about what you can bring to the business than having the perfect CV. You should be proactive, creative and genuinely interested in helping build something from an early stage.

We’d love to meet someone who...

  • Understands social media and how to build an engaged audience.
  • Can create, plan or direct compelling digital content.
  • Has a good instinct for branding, messaging and communication.
  • Thinks commercially and understands that marketing ultimately needs to drive growth.
  • Is comfortable testing ideas, learning from results and changing direction when necessary.
  • Can work independently and take genuine ownership of their side of the business.
  • Communicates well and enjoys collaborating on ideas and strategy.
  • Has the ambition and commitment to help build an early-stage company.

Great to have — but not essential

  • Previous marketing, social media, content or growth experience.
  • Experience launching or promoting an app, startup or digital product.
  • Knowledge of paid advertising or performance marketing.
  • Experience working with creators, influencers or online communities.
  • Basic understanding of analytics, conversion and user acquisition.
  • An existing network or audience that could help accelerate early growth.
🤝

IMPORTANT TO KNOW

A different kind of opportunity.

This is not a salaried job or traditional employment position. Appy Family is an early-stage, bootstrapped business and we're looking for someone who wants to join us as a genuine co-founder.

You'll be helping shape the business rather than simply carrying out a job description. That means bringing your own ideas, taking ownership of marketing and growth, and having a real voice in where Appy Family goes next.

There is currently no salary attached to the role. Any potential ownership or equity arrangement would be discussed openly with the right person and agreed as part of a formal co-founder agreement.

This could be right for you if: you're excited by the idea of getting involved early, building something from the ground up and helping create the value rather than joining once everything has already been built.

Privacy & control

Useful shouldn't mean unrestricted.

Our vision is not to give an AI unlimited access to someone's phone. Users should decide which services it can access, what it can prepare and what it can actually do.

Ask

The AI answers questions using information it is permitted to access.

Prepare

The AI prepares an action, such as a draft message or proposed event.

Confirm

The AI asks for permission before completing a sensitive action.

Automate

Authorised routine tasks could be performed automatically within user-defined limits.

Built to grow

One assistant. The services you already use.

The initial concept is mobile-first. Over time, the underlying approach could support a wider range of authorised services and workflows.

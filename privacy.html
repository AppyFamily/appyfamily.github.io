Nozey Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

AppyFamily ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Nozey mobile application.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Nozey collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

Location Data

Nozey requests your device location only to display crime information relevant to your current area or to the location you search for.

Your location is not stored on our servers.

Crime Data

Nozey displays publicly available crime information provided by the UK Police Data API. This information is supplied by UK policing organisations and is displayed for informational purposes only.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, Nozey may access:

  • Your device location (only with your permission)
  • Searches for UK postcodes or towns entered within the app
  • Basic technical information required for the app to function correctly

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-Party Services

Nozey uses third-party services including:

  • UK Police Data API
  • OpenStreetMap

These services have their own privacy policies.

Your Rights

If you are located in the UK or European Economic Area, you have rights regarding your personal information under applicable data protection laws, including the UK GDPR.
